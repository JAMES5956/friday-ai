## Reporting rules

1. Be accurate: separate facts, assumptions, estimates and recommendations.
2. Include evidence for completed work where possible.
3. Report blockers early; do not hide delays or failed checks.
4. Do not expose credentials, secrets or unnecessary personal information.
5. Do not contact third parties, publish, spend money or change production systems without required owner authorisation.
6. If the agent or department is not configured or operational, say so plainly.
